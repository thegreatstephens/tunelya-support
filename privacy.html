Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

TUNELYA is operated by Scott Stephens. This policy describes information processed to create, store, and share songs and operate your account. Contact thegreatstephens@aol.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information you provide

  • Account information: email, authentication information, and an account identifier, used to register, sign in, and secure your account.
  • Song content: ideas, lyrics, musical preferences, pronunciation guidance, and details you enter, such as names, stories, relationships, birth dates, and memorial dates.
  • Selected images: a photo you choose for a cover is processed and uploaded for that cover. Selecting a photo does not upload your whole photo library.
  • Activity and purchases: songs, library records, visibility choices, credit balances, purchase identifiers, and generation status, used to provide the service and avoid duplicate charges. Apple handles payments; TUNELYA does not receive full card details.
  • Support: your email, messages, attachments, and other information you choose to send.

How information is used

Information is used to generate songs, maintain accounts and libraries, track credits and verified purchases, provide sharing features, investigate reports, answer support requests, and protect the service. Do not include confidential information or other people’s information without permission.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database and cover storage, and server processing. fal routes music-generation requests to Mureka. fal and Mureka receive song instructions and lyrics—including personal details you place in them—to generate lyrics and audio. Generated audio is initially delivered through provider hosting. Cover photos are not included in music-generation requests. Apple handles App Store purchases and subscription billing.

TUNELYA asks fal not to retain request inputs and outputs in fal’s request-history interface. Because this Mureka endpoint is not currently designated Enterprise Ready by fal, fal’s enterprise no-training commitment and data-processing addendum do not apply. Mureka’s own retention and training practices are governed by Mureka’s published policies; TUNELYA has not received a separate promise that customer content is excluded from model improvement or training. Do not submit confidential information or content you do not want processed by these providers.

Opening provider-hosted audio connects to that provider. Providers may process technical data such as IP addresses and request logs under their policies. See Supabase’s privacy policy, fal’s API Services terms, Mureka’s privacy policy, and Apple’s privacy information.

Sharing and visibility

Publishing makes public song information available in discovery features. Consider whether lyrics, titles, and covers reveal personal details before publishing. Sending an audio link or exporting a file gives recipients access to that content. A private library setting does not revoke links or copies already shared.

Retention and deletion

Account and library information is retained while needed to provide the service. New songs are saved automatically on the device used to create them when local saving succeeds. Provider-hosted generated-file links are expected to remain available for 30 days, so download or share any song you want to keep within that period. A local copy can be lost if you delete TUNELYA, erase or replace the device, or remove its data. Keep a separate copy of important songs.

Request account deletion in the app or contact support. Deletion removes the account and associated active TUNELYA database records, uploaded covers, and account-specific cached data handled by the deletion flow. Unused credits are removed. Cancel Apple subscriptions separately to stop renewal charges.

Copies already downloaded or shared outside TUNELYA cannot be removed by us. Account deletion does not guarantee immediate deletion of provider-held inputs, provider-hosted audio, provider logs, or backups; those follow fal’s and Mureka’s retention processes. Mureka states that generated file URLs remain valid for 30 days and that uploaded files require a file-specific deletion request. Contact support for help with a provider-specific removal request. Apple retains purchase records under its policies. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve requests.

Tracking and security

TUNELYA does not include advertising tracking or sell personal information. Access controls protect account data, but no online service guarantees perfect security. Use a unique password and secure your email account.

This website uses GitHub Pages with no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or contact-form database. GitHub may process technical request information; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Support email uses your chosen email provider and our support mailbox.

Your choices

You choose the details and photos to submit and whether to publish or share. Contact support to request access, correction, or deletion. We may verify account ownership before acting. Rights and requirements vary by location.

Age requirement and other people’s information

You must be at least 18 years old to use TUNELYA’s music-generation service. Do not generate music through TUNELYA if you are under 18. Parents or guardians concerned about a young person’s information should contact support. Be careful when using another person’s photo, name, dates, or stories.

Updates and contact

Changes will be posted with an updated effective date. Questions: thegreatstephens@aol.com.
