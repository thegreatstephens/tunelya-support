Age requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to use TUNELYA’s music-generation service. By confirming your age and creating a song, you represent that you are at least 18.

Monthly plans

  • Creator: $9.99 per month, 7 credits each billing month, monthly-credit balance cap of 21.
  • Creator Plus: $19.99 per month, 16 credits each billing month, monthly-credit balance cap of 48.
  • Studio: $29.99 per month, 27 credits each billing month, monthly-credit balance cap of 81.

Unused monthly credits carry forward up to the plan’s cap. The cap equals three months’ allowance; it does not mean each credit expires exactly three months after issue. Purchased single-song credits are tracked separately.

One-time purchase

$2.99 for 1 song credit. This purchase does not renew. The credit does not expire while your TUNELYA account remains open.

Billing and cancellation

Prices shown are U.S. dollars. Apple shows your region’s price and applicable taxes before purchase. Monthly subscriptions charge your Apple Account and renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal may be charged within 24 hours before the period ends.

Manage or cancel through Apple subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal and does not automatically refund previous purchases. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel billing. Request refunds through Apple’s Report a Problem; Apple determines eligibility.

Song credits and AI results

Each new song generation uses one credit. A different generated version uses another credit. Explanations and reviewing choices do not use credits. Daily capacity or service interruptions can pause generation. A request rejected before generation starts does not use a credit. A successfully generated song uses one credit even if you dislike the style or performance.

AI sound, lyrics, pronunciation, singer, and duration may vary. These are directions rather than guarantees. If a request fails or credits look wrong, contact support with the approximate time and song details.

Saving your songs

New songs are saved automatically on the device used to create them when local saving succeeds. Provider-hosted generated-file links are expected to remain available for 30 days. Download or share each song you want to keep within 30 days and keep a separate backup before deleting TUNELYA, erasing or replacing your device, or removing the app’s data. Downloading and sharing do not use additional song credits.
