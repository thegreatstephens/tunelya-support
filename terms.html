Terms & Purchases

Updated September 26, 2026 · Launch information

TUNELYA uses Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (EULA) as its Terms of Use. Also read our Privacy Policy. The app is currently in testing; public purchases are not yet available.

Monthly plans

  • Creator: $9.99 per month, 12 credits each billing month, monthly-credit balance cap of 36.
  • Creator Plus: $19.99 per month, 28 credits each billing month, monthly-credit balance cap of 84.
  • Studio: $29.99 per month, 45 credits each billing month, monthly-credit balance cap of 135.

Unused monthly credits carry forward up to the plan’s cap. The cap equals three months’ allowance; it does not mean each credit expires exactly three months after issue. Pack credits are tracked separately.

One-time packs

$4.99 for 5 credits; $9.99 for 11 credits; $19.99 for 24 credits. Packs do not renew. Pack credits do not expire while your TUNELYA account remains open.

Billing and cancellation

Prices shown are U.S. dollars. Apple shows your region’s price and applicable taxes before purchase. Monthly subscriptions charge your Apple Account and renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal may be charged within 24 hours before the period ends.

Manage or cancel through Apple subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal and does not automatically refund previous purchases. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel billing. Request refunds through Apple’s Report a Problem; Apple determines eligibility.

Song credits and AI results

Each new song generation uses one credit. A different generated version uses another credit. Explanations and reviewing choices do not use credits. Daily capacity or service interruptions can pause generation. A request rejected before generation starts does not use a credit.

AI sound, lyrics, pronunciation, singer, and duration may vary. These are directions rather than guarantees. If a request fails or credits look wrong, contact support with the approximate time and song details.

Your content

Only submit lyrics, images, and personal information you have permission to use. Do not infringe rights, deceptively impersonate someone, harass people, or publish unlawful content. Generated music is not a guarantee of exclusive copyright or uniqueness. Commercial use must respect rights in the material you supply.

Deleting an account

Deletion removes your library records, uploaded covers, and unused credits. Save songs you want and separately cancel any subscription first. Downloaded or shared copies remain outside our control. See the Privacy Policy for retention details.
