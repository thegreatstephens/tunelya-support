A little guidance before you create.

You do not need to speak “music.” In-app explanations help you choose a mood and tempo. Review your choices before spending a credit.

Basic songs are aimed at around three minutes. AI results can vary in length, pronunciation, lyrics, and sound. A new generated version uses another credit.
